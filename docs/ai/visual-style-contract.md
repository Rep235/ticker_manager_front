# Visual Style Contract

This application follows a soft, layered, system-like visual language.

The UI must feel:
- Calm
- Spatial
- Precise
- Content-first
- Low visual noise

Visual decisions are intentional, not decorative.

---

## 1. Geometry

- Prefer rounded rectangles
- Border radius must be consistent and subtle
- Avoid sharp edges
- No exaggerated rounding

Guideline:
- Small elements: `rounded-md`
- Containers: `rounded-xl` or `rounded-2xl`

---

## 2. Spacing & Density

- Generous internal padding
- Clear separation between layers
- Avoid dense or compressed layouts

Rules:
- Padding > margins
- Space is part of the design
- Never crowd interactive elements

---

## 3. Color Usage

- Neutral base colors dominate
- Accent colors are used sparingly
- Avoid high saturation
- Avoid strong contrast unless for focus or error

UI should feel quiet, not loud.

---

## 4. Elevation & Depth

- Use subtle shadows to imply hierarchy
- No hard borders unless necessary
- Depth is suggested, not declared

Rules:
- Shadows must be soft and diffused
- Avoid dramatic elevation

---

## 5. Typography

- Clean, modern, humanist sans-serif
- Clear hierarchy
- No decorative fonts
- Avoid extreme font weights

Text should feel native and readable.

---

## 6. Interaction & Feedback

- Transitions must be subtle and fast
- Avoid dramatic animations
- Feedback should feel immediate and natural

Rules:
- Use opacity, scale, or shadow changes
- Avoid bounce or elastic effects

---

## 7. Visual Noise Reduction

- Avoid unnecessary icons
- Avoid excessive borders
- Avoid visual clutter

Every visual element must justify its existence.
